The world grid will consist of different types of cells:
1. Default cell
2. Ore cell
3. Water cell (optional)

The player will have an inventory to store resources. Chests will also be implemented.

Types of buildings:
1. Mining drill: Placed on a 3x3 grid area, it will collect all ores from "ore cells" within its range.
2. Crafting machine: Automates the crafting of various resources.
3. Furnace: Smelts raw ores into ingots to be used in future crafting.
4. Enemy lair: Randomly spawned across the world, this building will spawn enemies based on a cooldown timer.

Logistics and Engine Mechanics:
1. Conveyor logic will be implemented to move resources between different machines.
2. Conveyor throughput will be 1 item per second.
3. The player will have a gun to protect their base; ammunition for the gun must be crafted by the player.
4. The world grid will be generated using procedural generation.

Optional Features:
1. Add tiers and upgrade modules for buildings.
2. Add dual-lane conveyors and higher-tier conveyors with better throughput.
3. Add an electricity system for powering machines.
